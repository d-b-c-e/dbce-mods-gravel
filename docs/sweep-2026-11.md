## Issues

- **ISS-0039 (blocker):** the car won't pull away from a standstill while the brake pedal's resting reading (1.3%)
  holds it in a burnout. The mod passes the axis through unchanged, and the `WheelConfig.ini` fields are polarity,
  not dead zone. The likely fix is the brake pedal's calibration in MOZA Pit House or a brake dead zone in Gravel's own
  calibration, so the owner should check it.
